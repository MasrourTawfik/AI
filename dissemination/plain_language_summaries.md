## A Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) Decision Model for Heating Process Parameters Identification in Automotive Glass Manufacturing

**Plain-language summary**  
Industrial furnace settings are often chosen through expert experience and repeated trial and error. This work shows how a Deep Q-Network can learn suitable heating settings for tempered-glass manufacturing from desired process outputs. The goal is to reduce manual tuning while keeping the process within acceptable production limits.

**Why it matters**  
The study provides an early industrial example of deep reinforcement learning for manufacturing-process parameter configuration and creates a foundation for later work on autonomous and energy-aware process optimization.

**Keywords**  
deep reinforcement learning; DQN; industrial AI; smart manufacturing; process optimization; tempered glass

**DOI**  
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-51186-9_6

## Sparse smooth group L0∘L1/2 regularization method for convolutional neural networks

**Plain-language summary**  
Large convolutional neural networks often contain many redundant weights and filters. This paper proposes a structured regularization method that combines smooth approximations of L0 and L1/2 penalties so that entire groups of weights, as well as individual weights, can be driven toward sparsity during training.

**Why it matters**  
Structured sparsity can produce smaller and more efficient neural networks while preserving useful predictive performance, which is important for model compression and resource-constrained deployment.

**Keywords**  
CNN pruning; structured sparsity; neural network compression; L0 regularization; L1/2 regularization; sparse deep learning

**DOI**  
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knosys.2023.111327

## Towards an Improved CNN Architecture for Brain Tumor Classification

**Plain-language summary**  
This work compares established convolutional neural-network architectures for brain-tumor image classification and studies an alternative CNN architecture designed to obtain strong classification performance with a practical learning time.

**Why it matters**  
Brain-tumor classification is an important medical-imaging task. The study contributes to the continuing effort to balance classification quality, model complexity and training cost.

**Keywords**  
brain tumor classification; CNN; MRI; medical imaging; deep learning

**DOI**  
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-36778-7_24

## Deep Convolutional Neural Networks with Transfer Learning for Old Buildings Pathologies Automatic Detection

**Plain-language summary**  
Most automated building-defect studies focus mainly on cracks. This work uses transfer learning with deep CNNs to recognize seven different pathologies affecting old buildings, using images from the historic medinas of Fez and Meknes.

**Why it matters**  
Automated visual inspection can support faster and more systematic monitoring of architectural heritage, especially when expert inspection resources are limited.

**Keywords**  
heritage buildings; transfer learning; CNN; defect detection; structural pathology; computer vision

**DOI**  
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-36671-1_18

## Improved Hourly Prediction of BIPV Photovoltaic Power Building Using Artificial Learning Machine: A Case Study

**Plain-language summary**  
This study compares machine-learning approaches for short- and medium-term hourly prediction of electricity generated by building-integrated photovoltaic systems. Weather and irradiance variables are used to estimate future PV production.

**Why it matters**  
Accurate forecasting helps buildings and energy-management systems make better operational decisions and integrate variable renewable generation more effectively.

**Keywords**  
BIPV; photovoltaic forecasting; machine learning; renewable energy; smart buildings

**Canonical DOI**  
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-15191-0_26

## Optimizations of Distributed Computing Processes on Apache Spark Platform

**Plain-language summary**  
This work studies practical ways to reduce response time in Apache Spark big-data processing. The approach combines schema design, improved data distribution, memory caching and Parquet-based data materialization.

**Why it matters**  
Better Spark configuration can reduce computation time and improve resource use in large-scale data-analysis pipelines. The reported case study achieved a substantial reduction in response time.

**Keywords**  
Apache Spark; distributed computing; big data; caching; Parquet; performance optimization

**Publisher PDF**  
https://www.iaeng.org/IJCS/issues_v50/issue_2/IJCS_50_2_11.pdf

## A Review of Variational Inference for Bayesian Neural Network

**Plain-language summary**  
Standard neural networks usually provide point predictions without directly representing model uncertainty. This review explains how Bayesian neural networks and variational inference can be used to approximate probability distributions over model parameters and make uncertainty-aware predictions.

**Why it matters**  
Reliable uncertainty estimates are important when deep-learning systems are used in scientific, industrial or safety-sensitive decision making.

**Keywords**  
Bayesian neural networks; variational inference; uncertainty quantification; Bayesian deep learning

**DOI**  
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-43520-1_20
