# Mesures de défaut : observation et contrôle de plaques

**Authors:** Claude Bardos, Tawfik Masrour  
**Journal:** Comptes rendus de l'Académie des sciences. Série I, Mathématique  
**Volume:** 323  
**Issue:** 6  
**Pages:** 621–626  
**Year:** 1996

## Research summary

This note studies observation and control questions for plate equations through microlocal defect measures. It belongs to the mathematical-control line connecting propagation of high-frequency energy, observability inequalities, and exact controllability of PDEs.
