# Using Machine Learning for Predicting Efficiency in Manufacturing Industry

**Authors:** Choumicha El Mazgualdi, Tawfik Masrour, Ibtissam El Hassani, Abdelmoula Khdoudi  
**Venue:** Advanced Intelligent Systems for Sustainable Development (AI2SD’2019)  
**Pages:** 750–762  
**Publisher:** Springer  
**Year:** 2020  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-36671-1_68

## Research summary

This work investigates machine-learning prediction of Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) from real industrial production data. Several models are compared, including support vector methods, random forest, XGBoost and deep learning, with the objective of predicting equipment effectiveness early enough to support proactive manufacturing decisions.
