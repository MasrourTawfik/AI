# Boundary Exact Controllability of Elastodynamics Through Stiff or Soft Actuators

**Authors:** Frédéric Bourquin, Tawfik Masrour  
**Book:** Structural Control — Proceedings of the First European Conference  
**Page:** 116  
**Year:** 1997  
**Publisher:** World Scientific

## Research summary

This conference contribution addresses boundary exact controllability of elastodynamic systems through stiff or soft actuators. It belongs to the same mathematical-control program as the author's work on elastodynamic singularities, observability, and exact controllability of elastic waves.

## Keywords

Elastodynamics; boundary control; exact controllability; actuators; elastic waves; structural control.
