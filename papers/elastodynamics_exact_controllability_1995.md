# Condition nécessaire et suffisante pour la contrôlabilité exacte et la stabilisation du problème de l'élastodynamique

**Authors:** Claude Bardos, Tawfik Masrour, Frédéric Tatout  
**Journal:** Comptes rendus de l'Académie des sciences. Série I, Mathématique  
**Volume:** 320  
**Issue:** 10  
**Pages:** 1279–1281  
**Year:** 1995

## Research summary

This note adapts exact-controllability methods to the elastodynamic system and gives a necessary and sufficient condition for exact controllability and stabilization. It is part of the early mathematical-control work on elastic waves that was subsequently developed in the longer chapter *Observation and Control of Elastic Waves*.

## Selected later literature referencing this work

- Komornik-related and multiplier-method literature on controllability of waves and elasticity cites this note as an early elastodynamic control result.
- Dehman, B. & Raymond, J.-P. *Exact controllability for the Lamé system*. Mathematical Control and Related Fields 5(4), 743–760 (2015). https://doi.org/10.3934/mcrf.2015.5.743
