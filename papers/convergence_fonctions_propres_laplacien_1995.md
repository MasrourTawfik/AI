# Convergence des fonctions propres de troisième espèce pour le laplacien

**Author:** Tawfik Masrour  
**Journal:** Comptes rendus de l'Académie des sciences. Série I, Mathématique  
**Volume:** 321  
**Issue:** 3  
**Pages:** 309–312  
**Year:** 1995

## Research summary

This note concerns the convergence of eigenfunctions of the Laplacian under third-kind (Robin/Wentzell-type) boundary conditions. It is part of the author's early work on spectral questions, boundary conditions, and controllability of distributed systems.
