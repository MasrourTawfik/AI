# Sparse smooth group L0∘L1/2 regularization method for convolutional neural networks

**Authors:** Mohamed Quasdane, Hassan Ramchoun, Tawfik Masrour  
**Journal:** Knowledge-Based Systems  
**Volume:** 284  
**Article:** 111327  
**Year:** 2024  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knosys.2023.111327

## Research summary

This paper proposes a structured sparse regularization approach for convolutional neural networks based on a composition of L0 and L1/2 penalties. Because the original penalties are nonsmooth, the method uses smooth approximations suitable for gradient-based learning. The objective is to induce both group-level and weight-level sparsity and thereby obtain more compact CNNs while retaining predictive performance.

Experiments on benchmark datasets and multiple CNN architectures compare the proposed method with established sparse regularization approaches.

## Keywords

CNN pruning; structured sparsity; neural network compression; L0 regularization; L1/2 regularization; sparse deep learning; model compression.
