# Deep Convolutional Neural Networks with Transfer Learning for Old Buildings Pathologies Automatic Detection

**Authors:** Tawfik Masrour, Ibtissam El Hassani, Mohammed Salim Bouchama  
**Venue:** Advanced Intelligent Systems for Sustainable Development (AI2SD’2019)  
**Pages:** 204–216  
**Publisher:** Springer  
**Year:** 2020  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-36671-1_18

## Research summary

This work applies transfer learning with deep convolutional neural networks to automatic recognition of several pathologies affecting old buildings. Unlike approaches limited mainly to crack detection, the study considers multiple deterioration categories encountered in historic urban fabric.

The case study uses imagery from old buildings in the medinas of Fez and Meknes and evaluates pretrained CNN architectures on a multi-class pathology-recognition task.

## Keywords

Transfer learning; deep CNN; heritage buildings; old buildings; structural pathology; defect detection; computer vision.
