**Book:** Singularities and Oscillations  
**Series:** IMA Volumes in Mathematics and its Applications, Vol. 91  
**Pages:** 1–16  
**Publisher:** Springer New York  
**Year:** 1997  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4612-1972-9_1

## Research summary

This chapter extends observation and control results for wave equations to the elastic-wave system under physical stress-free boundary conditions. The analysis uses the equations satisfied by the divergence and curl of the elastic displacement together with microlocal propagation of the wave front set.

The work belongs to the mathematical control and microlocal-analysis line developed around exact observability and controllability of hyperbolic and elastodynamic systems.
