# Singularités du problème d'élastodynamique

**Authors:** Claude Bardos, Tawfik Masrour, Frédéric Tatout  
**Journal:** Comptes rendus de l'Académie des sciences. Série I, Mathématique  
**Volume:** 320  
**Issue:** 9  
**Pages:** 1157–1160  
**Year:** 1995

## Research summary

This note studies the propagation and reflection of microlocal singularities for the elastodynamic problem with free boundary conditions. It forms part of the early mathematical-control and microlocal-analysis work that led to later exact controllability results for elastic-wave systems.
