# Gaussian mixture models for training Bayesian convolutional neural networks

**Authors:** Mostafa Bakhouya, Hassan Ramchoun, Mohammed Hadda, Tawfik Masrour  
**Journal:** Evolutionary Intelligence  
**Volume:** 17  
**Issue:** 4  
**Pages:** 2515–2536  
**Year:** 2024  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s12065-023-00900-9

## Research summary

This paper extends Bayes by Backprop by using a Gaussian-mixture variational distribution for Bayesian convolutional neural networks. The approach is designed to represent multimodal posterior structure more flexibly than standard unimodal approximations while retaining a reparameterization strategy compatible with gradient-based training.

The study evaluates the method on classification benchmarks and also considers epistemic and aleatoric uncertainty estimation.
